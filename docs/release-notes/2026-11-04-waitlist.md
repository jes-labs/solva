# The waitlist keeps a list, confirms each signup, and accepts each address once

Joining the waitlist now saves the person to a "Waitlist" segment in Resend, with their organisation and role, and sends them a confirmation from `hello@send.joinsolva.xyz` with `hello@joinsolva.xyz` as reply-to. The team no longer gets an email per signup; it reads the list in Resend. An address already on the list sees "This email is already on the waitlist." and nothing is saved or sent.

## Changes

- `features/contact/server/waitlist.ts`: look up the address in Resend Contacts, create the contact in the segment, send the confirmation. A failed lookup or create shows the form's error state; a failed confirmation after the contact is saved is logged and the person stays on the list.
- `RESEND_WAITLIST_SEGMENT_ID` is read with `RESEND_API_KEY`; without either, the waitlist reports itself as unconfigured.
- Outgoing messages carry their recipient; the contact form still emails the team inbox with the sender as reply-to.
- The waitlist's success panel says a confirmation was sent.

## Removed

- The team notification email for waitlist signups (`waitlistMessage`).

## Verification

- Red, then green: new tests for a new address, an address already listed, a failed lookup, a failed create, a failed confirmation, a missing segment id, the honeypot and a missing role; env tests for the segment id.
- After deploy: one signup appears in the "Waitlist" segment with its properties, the address receives the confirmation, and a second signup with the same address is refused.
