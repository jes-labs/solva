# Solva's look, refined

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Technical version:** [ADR-2026-11-01-brand-system.md](ADR-2026-11-01-brand-system.md)

## What is the problem?

Solva's yellow-green colour and logo are good and recognisable, but some text is too faint to read comfortably, the colours do not cover the three answers Solva gives (covered, short, stale), and the bright colour is used everywhere, which makes the brand look like a tool for programmers.

## What are we doing about it?

We keep the colour and the logo. The yellow-green is used only where it means something: the main button and the moment a result is confirmed. Covered, short and stale each get their own colour and shape. The logo gets a ring of small ticks around it, like a seal, which is the same seal shown large on the website.

## What changes for people?

The website and the docs get the refined look. Logo files, the link preview image and our social banners are redone without the "zero-knowledge" line. The dashboard keeps its current look for now.

## What could go wrong?

At the smallest browser-tab size the ticks may blur. If they do, the tab icon shows the logo mark alone.
