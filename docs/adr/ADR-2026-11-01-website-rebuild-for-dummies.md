# A new joinsolva.xyz that sells Solva

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Technical version:** [ADR-2026-11-01-website-rebuild.md](ADR-2026-11-01-website-rebuild.md)

## What is the problem?

Our website was made for a hackathon. It talks to programmers, not to the companies we want as customers, and some of what it says is not true yet, like made-up uptime numbers and a list of banks that are not our customers.

## What are we doing about it?

We are building a brand-new website for two kinds of customers: teams that issue currency tokens on Stellar, and apps that hold their customers' money. In plain language it explains what Solva is, what it does for them, and why their customers, partners and auditors can trust the result. It has a page for each kind of customer, a guided demo, a short study of the market, and a clear path from test network to launch. Technical detail goes to the docs. It does not link to the dashboard.

## What changes for people?

- Visitors see a calm, professional site in Solva's refined colours, with the new seal logo.
- Every number on the site says where it came from and when.
- Old links keep working and forward to the new pages.
- The pricing and status pages go away until we have real ones.

## What could go wrong?

A new site takes time to build and check before the 8 November deadline. We review it page by page on a private preview before it replaces the live site.

## What changed after the reviews

- The first version looked too much like another company's site. It was rebuilt as a ruled ledger that belongs to Solva: a wide page between two thin lines, the Seal's tick ring as the one picture, and the coverage record shown as a document.
- The site now moves: sections appear as you scroll, the ring draws itself, numbers count up, and the home chapter that makes a record pins while you scroll through its five steps. If your device asks for less motion, none of it moves.
- The site no longer explains how the proof is built. That belongs in the documentation. The pages say what a record proves, what it hides and who checks it.
- Every sentence was rewritten by hand so it reads clearly and sounds like a person wrote it.
