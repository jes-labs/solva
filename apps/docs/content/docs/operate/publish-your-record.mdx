---
title: Publish your record
description: Link your coverage record from stellar.toml with SEP-1 attestation_of_reserve, from your site, and to your partners.
---

Your record already lives in your registry on Stellar. Point holders and partners at it from your `stellar.toml`, from your own site and in what you send partners.

## Link it from stellar.toml

SEP-1 defines `stellar.toml`, the file an institution serves at `https://<your-domain>/.well-known/stellar.toml` to describe its tokens. Your Stellar account's home domain ties the file to your account. For each token, the `[[CURRENCIES]]` table has an `attestation_of_reserve` field: a URL to "attestation or other proof, evidence, or verification of reserves". Point it at your record.

Use the address of your registry on a Stellar explorer. Public record pages <ComingSoon /> give each token a page at a stable address on joinsolva.xyz, with the latest result and its history. Once your page exists, point the URL there.

<Steps>
  <Step>
    Find your registry's contract id. The deploy command in [Set up your institution](/docs/operate/set-up-your-institution) printed it.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Add `attestation_of_reserve` to the `[[CURRENCIES]]` entry of each token the record covers.

    ```toml title="stellar.toml"
    [[CURRENCIES]]
    code = "ARSK"
    issuer = "<your issuing account>"
    status = "test"
    is_asset_anchored = true
    anchor_asset_type = "fiat"
    anchor_asset = "ARS"
    attestation_of_reserve = "https://stellar.expert/explorer/testnet/contract/CAFUK25F56VW442VAOHDKGMPYF2BZ3ERVLVAM4H6JTN2WGZPDUQLRTPN"
    ```

    This example uses the Testnet registry of Kestrel Anchor, a fictional example institution. Use your own contract id. Use `status = "test"` while you are on Testnet.

  </Step>
  <Step>
    Publish the file. SEP-1 requires the header `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` and a file under 100 KB.
  </Step>
  <Step>
    Open `https://<your-domain>/.well-known/stellar.toml` and follow the link to check that it reaches your registry.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  SEP-1 also has `collateral_addresses` with signed messages. Those prove that you control an
  address. They say nothing about its balance, so they are not a substitute for a coverage record.
</Callout>

## Link it from your site

Put the same link where holders look for backing: your token page, your transparency or reserves page, and your exchange listings. Say what the record is in one line, for example: "A daily proof on Stellar that our reserves cover what we owe customers. No customer balance is shown."

Tell holders they can [check that their own balance was counted](/docs/verify/check-a-balance-was-counted). The check needs the record's id, the holder's id hash and balance, and their path through the tree. The orchestrator returns these for one customer at `GET /v1/proofs/inclusion/{proof-uuid}:{id-hash}`, and the registry's `verify_inclusion` confirms them on chain.

## Tell your partners

Send each partner, such as a wallet, an exchange, a lender or your auditor:

| Item               | Example                                                    |
| ------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Registry contract  | `CAFUK25F56VW442VAOHDKGMPYF2BZ3ERVLVAM4H6JTN2WGZPDUQLRTPN` |
| Network            | Stellar Testnet                                            |
| Tokens covered     | ARSK                                                       |
| Cycle schedule     | Once a day, after 00:00 UTC                                |
| Unit of the totals | Minor units of ARS, two decimals                           |

A partner reads the latest record from the registry, with no account at Solva:

```bash
stellar contract invoke \
  --id CAFUK25F56VW442VAOHDKGMPYF2BZ3ERVLVAM4H6JTN2WGZPDUQLRTPN \
  --source-account <any-account> \
  --network testnet \
  --send=no \
  -- get_latest_proof
```

The result holds the reserves total `r`, the total owed `l`, the tree's root `root_h` and the ledger `timestamp`. For what each field means, see [What a record shows](/docs/overview/records).

## Result

Holders find your record from your `stellar.toml` and your site, and partners read it from Stellar themselves.
