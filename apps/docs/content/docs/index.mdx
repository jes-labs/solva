Solva is an open reserve-coverage registry on Stellar. An anchor or a custodial app proves, with a zero-knowledge proof, that its reserves cover what it owes its customers. A contract on Stellar checks the proof and keeps the record. Anyone can read it, and every holder can check that their own balance was counted.

If you are new to Solva, start with [What is Solva](/docs/overview/what-is-solva).

## See it work

The demo at [joinsolva.xyz/demo](https://joinsolva.xyz/demo) runs the real pipeline on Stellar Testnet. It uses two example institutions, Kestrel Anchor and Solstice Exchange, and publishes a proof to a registry you can open on a Stellar explorer.
